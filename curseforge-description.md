Walk into Goldshire and you get Goldshire. Walk into Northshire Abbey and you get the Abbey: a clerical order the First War nearly finished off, and the archbishop who rebuilt it and then founded the Knights of the Silver Hand.

Most lore addons work at zone level. This one goes down to the town, the mine, the ruin and the dungeon interior, because that is the scale you play at.

**626 places written up:** 50 zones, 562 towns, landmarks and ruins, and 14 dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.

## How it works

A small card fades in when you arrive somewhere new, carrying one line about where you are. Click it for the full write-up: a few paragraphs, a curiosity worth knowing, and the wiki articles behind it. Ignore the card and it fades out by itself.

Prefer silence? Turn the card off and type `/ftn` when you want it. There is a minimap button as well.

## What the lore covers

Everything stops at **patch 1.12**. Warcraft I, II and III and the pre-WoW history are in scope: the Sundering, the War of the Ancients, Arathor, the War of the Three Hammers, the fall of Lordaeron, the Battle of Mount Hyjal. Nothing from The Burning Crusade onward.

It is written in the present tense of vanilla. Varian is missing. Dalaran is a crater. Onyxia sits on Stormwind's regency council as Lady Katrana Prestor and nobody has worked it out.

**No spoilers.** Entries give you the standing situation and the history under it. They never tell you how a questline resolves or what is waiting at the bottom of the dungeon you are walking into. "The Scarlet Crusade's leadership here is not what it claims to be" is as far as any of them go.

### Forever's new zones

Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle have entries, and they are **off by default**. Switch them on with `/ftn forever on` and you get background only: where the place sits, who lives there, and the older history its name reaches back to. Walk into Hyjal already knowing what Nordrassil was and what the night elves spent to destroy Archimonde. Nothing about the new story.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/ftn` | read about where you are |
| `/ftn <place>` | look anything up by name, e.g. `/ftn stratholme` |
| `/ftn list` | every zone with an entry |
| `/ftn list Kalimdor` | one continent |
| `/ftn config` | settings |
| `/ftn forever on` | include Forever's new zones |
| `/ftn card off` | stop the arrival card, keep `/ftn` |
| `/ftn stats` | how much of the world you have been told about |
| `/ftn where` | print the zone strings the client reports |

## Settings worth knowing

- **Only the first time I ever visit.** Off by default. Switch it on and each place speaks up once per character, ever, instead of once every fifteen minutes.
- **Quiet on flight paths.** On by default. A taxi route crosses dozens of places in two minutes. None are announced, and none are recorded as visited, so flying over somewhere does not spend its first visit.
- **Quiet in combat.** The card waits until the fight ends.
- **Zones, towns and dungeons separately.** Three switches, so you can keep the landmark detail after you have learnt the zone-level entries.
- **Open the panel instead of the card**, for when you are reading rather than levelling.

## Works on

World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.1) and Classic Era (1.15.x), from the same download. Not retail.

## Accuracy

Every lore claim was checked against the community wiki at warcraft.wiki.gg. Seventeen turned out to be wrong or unsupported and were corrected before release, among them the Dark Irons never having held Grim Batol, the Stonemasons' Guild never having rebuilt Northshire Abbey, and Un'Goro's crystal pylons having no attributed builder at all.

Each write-up links the articles behind it. The game cannot open a browser, so clicking a link hands you the address in a box with the text already selected.

## Something I need

Forever's four new zones are thin: Mount Hyjal has two entries, Riverglades one, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle none. Reliable subzone names for them do not exist outside the game, and guessing at spellings would produce entries that never match what the client reports.

Stand somewhere named in one of those zones and run `/ftn where`. It prints exactly what the client calls that spot. Post those strings and they will get proper entries.
